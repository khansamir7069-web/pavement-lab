Final pavement designs, thickness profiles, and mix configurations must be independently verified, reviewed, and signed off by a licensed and qualified Professional Pavement Engineer before field execution, bidding, or construction.

---

## 1. Project Background
This demo workflow walks through the pavement design of the **National Highway NH-99 Corridor** (4-lane divided carriageway, subgrade soaked CBR of 4.0%). The goal is to design a structurally sound flexible pavement with stabilized layers to optimize material usage and minimize bituminous cover thickness.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### 2.1 Project Creation
1.  Open the Project Hub and click **Create New Project**.
2.  Set:
    *   **Work Name:** `NH-99 4-Lane Divided Carriageway`
    *   **Client:** `NHAI Regional Office`
    *   **Consultant Name:** `Pavement Consultants Group`
    *   **Submitted By:** `QA Lead Auditor`
    *   **Mix Type:** `DBM-II`
    *   **Binder Grade:** `VG-30`
3.  Click **Create Project**.

---

### 2.2 Traffic count and MSA Calculation
1.  Go to the **Traffic Analysis** tab.
2.  Input raw traffic survey data:
    *   **Initial Commercial Traffic (CVPD):** `2500` (derived from 7-day volume counts)
    *   **Annual Traffic Growth Rate (%):** `6.5`
    *   **Design Life (years):** `15`
    *   **Vehicle Damage Factor (VDF):** `4.5` (from axle-load survey)
    *   **Lane Distribution Factor (LDF):** `0.75` (typical for 4-lane divided carriageways, per IRC:37 cl. 4.4)
3.  Click **Compute Traffic**.
    *   *Result:* Cumulative traffic calculated = **66.25 MSA**.
    *   *Growth Factor:* **23.716**.
4.  Click **Save & Continue**.

---

### 2.3 Subgrade CBR and Modulus calculation
1.  Go to the **Subgrade Properties** tab.
2.  Input laboratory soaked CBR values:
    *   **Subgrade Soaked CBR (%):** `4.0`
3.  Click **Calculate Modulus**.
    *   *Result:* Modulus ($M_r$) = **40.0 MPa** (calculated as $10 \times 4.0\%$, since CBR $\le 5\%$).
4.  Click **Save**.

---

### 2.4 Catalogue design lookup
1.  Go to the **Catalogue Design** tab.
2.  Click **Lookup Catalogue Composition**.
    *   *Result:* The catalog query finds the closest match for **CBR 3-5%, MSA 50-100**:
        *   Bituminous Concrete (BC): `40 mm`
        *   Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM): `170 mm`
        *   Wet Mix Macadam (WMM): `250 mm`
        *   Granular Sub-base (GSB): `300 mm`
3.  Click **Adopt Suggestion** to load this into the Structural design workspace.

---

### 2.5 Stabilized Design Wizard (CTB/CTS Option)
1.  Go to the **Stabilized Design** tab.
2.  Input stabilized layer target values:
    *   **Cement-Treated Base (CTB) Thickness:** `120 mm`
    *   **CTB Modulus:** `5000 MPa`
    *   **CTB 28-day UCS Strength:** `4.5 MPa`
    *   **Cement-Treated Sub-base (CTS) Thickness:** `100 mm`
    *   **CTS Modulus:** `3000 MPa`
    *   **Granular Sub-base (GSB) Thickness:** `150 mm`
    *   **Bituminous Cover Thickness:** `100 mm`
3.  Click **Calculate Stabilized Composition**.
    *   *Result:* Nominal bituminous thickness savings = **110 mm** (a 52% saving in expensive asphalt binders).
    *   *Comparison mode:* Decision Support Mode (unless `IITPAVE.exe` is configured).

---

### 2.6 Engineering Intelligence Screen
1.  Navigate to the **Structural Pavement Design** tab.
2.  Review the **Engineering Screening Score** and warnings on the right side:
    *   *Stiff Layer over Weak Support:* CTB is placed directly over GSB. Since GSB modulus is 150 MPa ($< 500$ MPa), this triggers a warning: *"Very stiff stabilized layer 'CTB' is placed directly over weak support. Potential risk of cracking due to stiffness mismatch."*
    *   *Stiffness Inversion:* Reviewed; layers follow structural tier order.
    *   *Screening Score:* Displays `65/100` (Needs engineering review).
3.  Review findings and note in the design diary that shrinkage interlayers will be specified.

---

### 2.7 IITPAVE Mechanistic Verification (If exe configured)
1.  If `IITPAVE.exe` is configured in the **IITPAVE Manager**, click **Run Mechanistic Validation**.
2.  The engine runs in the background and parses the strain outputs:
    *   Fatigue tensile strain ($\varepsilon_t$) at bottom of bituminous layer.
    *   Vertical compressive strain ($\varepsilon_v$) at top of subgrade.
3.  The system confirms if fatigue and rutting lives exceed design requirements.

---

### 2.8 Review checklist and Project Locking
1.  Navigate to the **Submission Center**.
2.  Complete the **Consultant Checklist**:
    *   Check: *Traffic counts verified*
    *   Check: *Subgrade soil soaked CBR test verified*
    *   Check: *Layer modular ratios reviewed*
3.  Set review status to **Reviewed**.
4.  Click **Lock Project** to secure the design parameters from modification.

---

### 2.9 DPR Export and Submission ZIP Package
1.  Click **Export DPR**. Choose file name `NH_99_Detailed_Project_Report.docx` and click **Save**.
2.  Click **Export Package (.zip)**. Choose file name `NH_99_Submission_Package.zip` and click **Save**.
3.  The ZIP package compiles:
    *   `NH_99_Detailed_Project_Report.docx` (Word DPR)
    *   `ProjectInputs.json` (Database snapshot)
    *   `README_Disclaimer.txt` (Disclaimer text)
    *   `ApprovalSheet.txt` (Checks sheet)

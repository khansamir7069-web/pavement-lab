# RoadX Design Auditor & Checker Validation Checklist

## CRITICAL SAFETY DISCLAIMER
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **RoadX is a professional decision-support engineering tool.** All empirical design projections, thickness suggestions, material specifications, and mechanistic calculations generated by this software are for engineering screening and decision-support only. Final pavement designs, thickness profiles, and mix configurations must be independently verified, reviewed, and signed off by a licensed and qualified Professional Pavement Engineer before field execution, bidding, or construction.

---

This checklist is designed for senior design auditors, peer checkers, and government examiners to verify the integrity of pavement designs processed using the RoadX Engineering Suite.

---

## 1. Input Verification Check
*   [ ] **Classified Traffic Volume Counts:** Verify that initial CVPD is sourced from a 7-day volume count survey (per IRC:9) and has been adjusted for the years between count and construction completion.
*   [ ] **Traffic Growth Rate:** Confirm the annual growth rate ($r\%$) matches local masterplans or econometric projections. Watch out for unrealistically high projections ($> 8.0\%$).
*   [ ] **Design Life ($n$):** Verify design life matches road category specifications (typically 15 years for National Highways/State Highways, 20 years for expressways).
*   [ ] **Vehicle Damage Factor (VDF):** Cross-check VDF against regional axle-load survey studies. If default tables are used, confirm the traffic volume class matches MORTH guidelines.
*   [ ] **Lane Distribution Factor (LDF):** Confirm LDF matches road configuration (e.g. 0.75 for 4-lane divided carriageway).
*   [ ] **Soaked CBR:** Confirm the subgrade soil CBR value represents a laboratory 4-day soaked test (per IS:2720 Part 16) on the worst-performing borrow-pit soil samples.

---

## 2. Structural Layer Compatibility Check
*   [ ] **Base-to-Subbase Modular Ratio:** Confirm that the elastic modulus ratio between WMM (granular base) and GSB (granular sub-base) is between 2.0 and 4.0.
*   [ ] **Stiffness Inversion:** Verify that lower layers are not stiffer than upper layers (e.g., granular sub-base modulus must not exceed granular base modulus).
*   *Exception:* Bituminous cover over a Cement-Treated Base (CTB) slab is not considered a stiffness inversion.
*   [ ] **Stiff CTB Over Granular Layer:** If a Cement-Treated Base ($E \ge 5000$ MPa) is specified, confirm that the underlying sub-base is either a Cement-Treated Sub-base (CTS) or a high-strength GSB ($E \ge 500$ MPa). If placed directly on weak support ($E < 500$ MPa), confirm shrinkage-relief measures are detailed.
*   [ ] **Poisson's Ratio:** Confirm Poisson's ratios conform to typical elastic limits:
    *   Bituminous Concrete / DBM: 0.35
    *   Wet Mix Macadam / Granular Base: 0.35
    *   Cement-Treated Base / Sub-base: 0.25
    *   Subgrade Soil: 0.35

---

## 3. Mix Design Volumetrics & OBC Check
*   [ ] **Air Voids ($V_a$):** Confirm air voids at the designated Optimum Binder Content (OBC) fall within the target design specifications (3.0% to 5.0%, typically 4.0% for DBM/BC).
*   [ ] **Voids in Mineral Aggregate ($VMA$):** Verify $VMA$ complies with minimum requirements (typically $\ge 12.0\%$ for DBM with 37.5mm nominal size and $\ge 13.0\%$ for 26.5mm size, per MORTH Table 500-11).
*   [ ] **Voids Filled with Bitumen ($VFB$):** Check that $VFB$ is within the specified range (65% to 75% for DBM/BC).
*   [ ] **Stability & Flow:** Verify stability is $> 9.0$ kN and flow is between 2.0 mm and 4.0 mm.

---

## 4. IITPAVE Mechanistic Verification
*   [ ] **Production Mode:** Confirm the design has been processed in **Mechanistic Verified Mode** (via local `IITPAVE.exe` runs) rather than Decision Support fallback mode.
*   [ ] **Tensile Strain ($\varepsilon_t$):** Confirm the tensile strain at the bottom of the bituminous layer does not exceed the fatigue life design strain.
*   [ ] **Vertical Compressive Strain ($\varepsilon_v$):** Confirm the vertical strain at the top of the subgrade does not exceed the rutting life design strain.

---

## 5. Review Checklist and Project Sign-Off
*   [ ] **Project Lock State:** Verify the project is **Locked** in the Submission Center to prevent further modifications.
*   [ ] **Submission ZIP Package:** Verify the ZIP archive exists and contains all required output logs and calculations.
*   [ ] **Independent Engineer Sign-off:** Ensure the final report bears the manual signature and seal of a licensed Professional Pavement Engineer.
